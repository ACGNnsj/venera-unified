# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

适用产品：Venera Unified（Windows / Android / iOS / macOS / Linux）
最后更新：2026-11-03

## 中文

### 1. 我们不运营服务器
本应用是本地优先的漫画阅读器。我们不运营任何用于收集用户数据的服务器，也没有账号系统：你无需注册即可使用，我们不会收到你的姓名、邮箱、手机号、位置或设备标识符。

### 2. 数据保存在你自己的设备上
漫画源、书库、阅读进度、收藏、历史、设置与图片缓存全部写入本机应用数据目录（数据库与缓存文件）。这些数据不会上传给我们。

### 3. 应用会在什么情况下联网
- 访问你自行添加或启用的漫画源网站，以获取封面、目录与图片；这些请求直接发往对应网站。
- 检查应用更新：向 GitHub Releases 查询最新版本与发布说明。
- 你主动开启的 WebDAV 备份/同步：数据发送到你填写的、属于你自己的服务器。
- 你在应用内打开外部链接时，交由系统浏览器处理。
上述各第三方网站按其自身的隐私政策处理请求信息（例如 IP、User-Agent），我们无法控制。

### 4. 权限用途
- 存储/文件访问：导入本地漫画、导出备份、选择保存位置。仅在你主动选择文件或目录时使用。
- 相机：扫描二维码以导入配置。图像在本机解析，不上传。
- 生物识别/系统锁屏：用于应用锁。校验由操作系统完成，我们不接触你的指纹或面部数据。
- 通知/后台运行：仅用于你开启的同步与更新提醒。

### 5. 不投放广告、不做统计分析
本应用不包含广告 SDK，也不集成第三方统计、埋点或崩溃上报服务。应用内的诊断信息（阅读性能等）只写在本机，需你主动导出。

### 6. 儿童
本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

### 7. 数据删除
卸载应用，或在系统设置中清除应用数据即可删除本机全部数据；WebDAV 上的备份需你在自己的服务器上删除。

### 8. 变更与联系
本政策更新后会随版本发布。如有疑问，请通过应用商店页面所列联系方式或项目仓库的 Issue 反馈。

## English

### 1. No servers, no accounts
Venera Unified is a local-first comic reader. We operate no server that collects user data and there is no account system: no sign-up is required, and we do not receive your name, email, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

### 2. Your data stays on your device
Comic sources, library, reading progress, favorites, history, settings and image caches are written to the app data directory on your device and are never uploaded to us.

### 3. When the app uses the network
- It requests covers, chapter lists and images from the comic source websites you add or enable; those requests go directly to those sites.
- It checks for updates via GitHub Releases.
- If you enable WebDAV backup/sync, data is sent only to the server address you configure yourself.
- External links you open are handed to your system browser.
Third-party sites process request metadata (such as IP address and User-Agent) under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

### 4. Permissions
- Storage/file access: importing local comics, exporting backups, choosing save locations. Used only when you pick a file or folder.
- Camera: scanning QR codes to import configuration. Images are parsed on-device and are not uploaded.
- Biometrics/system lock: used for the app lock. Verification is performed by the operating system; we never access your fingerprint or face data.
- Notifications/background execution: only for sync and update prompts you enable.

### 5. No ads, no analytics
The app contains no advertising SDK and integrates no third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting service. On-device diagnostics (such as reading performance) stay on your machine and are exported only when you ask.

### 6. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

### 7. Deleting data
Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system settings removes all on-device data. Backups stored on your WebDAV server must be deleted there by you.

### 8. Changes and contact
Updates to this policy ship with app releases. For questions, use the contact details on the store listing or open an issue in the project repository.
